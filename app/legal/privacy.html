FPLabs ("the app", "we") is a Fantasy Premier League planning tool operated by KoalaaDev ("the operator"). This policy explains what the app stores about you, why, for how long, and how to remove it. It is written to be read, not skimmed: there is no advertising, no analytics tracking, and no sale or sharing of personal data with third parties for their own purposes.

Signing in is optional. Everything the app does works without an account; an account only makes your saved work follow you between devices.

2. What we do not collect

  • No password: authentication is delegated to Google and we never see one.
  • No payment details: nothing is charged for at present.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers, no fingerprinting, no third-party cookies.
  • Your Fantasy Premier League login: the recommended way to load a private squad is a bookmarklet that runs in your own browser and copies the squad to your clipboard. Your FPL session cookie never reaches our server unless the operator has explicitly enabled the optional cookie import, in which case it is used for one request and never stored.

3. Cookies

Two first-party cookies only, both essential, both HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax:

  • fplabs_session: a signed session token (30 days). It contains an opaque session id and, if signed in, an opaque account id. Nothing else.
  • fplabs_oauth: a short-lived (10 minute) token used only to complete a Google sign-in securely.

The app also remembers your team id in your browser's local storage so the page loads faster. It is never sent anywhere except to this site.

4. Third parties

  • Google Sign-In. If you choose to sign in, Google receives the sign-in request and returns your basic profile (name, email, avatar). Google's own privacy policy applies to that exchange. We request only the openid, email and profile scopes.
  • Google Fonts. The page loads two typefaces from Google's font service; your browser makes that request directly to Google.
  • Fantasy Premier League and other public data sources. The app's server fetches public football data (fixtures, player statistics, public team picks by team id) to build its projections. Those requests are made by the server on its own behalf and do not include your personal data beyond the public team id you entered.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone else. We would disclose data only where required by law.

5. Where data is kept and how it is protected

Account data and saved documents live in a database on the operator's server, reachable only over HTTPS. Session tokens are cryptographically signed so they cannot be forged or altered, and every state-changing request is protected against cross-site forgery. Rate limiting, request size limits and content security headers are applied to every response.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability. Everything stored about you is visible in the app (your team id, squad, drafts and notes). You can ask for a copy at the contact address below.
  • Deletion. Signed-in users can delete their account from the account menu; this removes the Google identifiers, your email and name, and every saved document, immediately and permanently. Anonymous sessions are removed by clearing the site's cookies, and their saved documents expire automatically.
  • Sign out ends the session on that device without deleting the account.
  • Objection and complaints. If you are in the UK or EU you may also complain to your data-protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Fantasy Premier League itself requires its participants to be at least 13.
